What Is the PXRACK Under Sink Organizer?

The PXRACK Under Sink Organizer is a two-tier storage system built to sit beneath a sink, with a thickened metal frame and a wooden-style drawer handle on each sliding shelf. The shelves pull out on sliders, so you can reach items stored toward the back without taking everything out first.

The frame uses a C-shaped layout, leaving open space around the middle for sink plumbing, and the height adjusts across 5 settings — from about 13.6" to 16.9" — so the same unit can fit a range of cabinet sizes. This listing includes two organizers, so you could use one under the kitchen sink and the other in a bathroom, laundry room, or pantry, or place both side by side for more storage in one spot.

Before You Buy: What to Check

A quick measurement now can save you a return later. Before ordering, it's worth checking:

  • Cabinet width and depth Compare your under-sink cabinet's inside width and depth to the listed footprint of 15.9"D × 11.8"W.
  • Available height Measure the clear space from the cabinet floor to the underside of the sink basin, and compare it to the 13.6"–16.9" adjustable range.
  • Pipe position The C-shaped frame is designed to leave room around plumbing, but it's worth checking where your own pipes sit before assuming a perfect fit.
  • What you plan to store The listing notes that the space below the upper basket can fit bottles up to about 13" tall.
